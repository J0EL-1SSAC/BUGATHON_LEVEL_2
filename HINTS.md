# Level 2 — Progressive hints

Release one at a time.

1. You already have access to the machine. Start by understanding who you are and what that account can see.
2. Operational shortcuts often leave credentials somewhere less formal than an authentication service.
3. After changing users, compare the local environment and service information you can now inspect.
4. The records application is internal for a reason. Look for the intended staff access route.
5. A record lookup that reacts strangely to unexpected input may be revealing implementation detail.
6. If a query can be influenced, the application’s visible data is only a subset of what the database knows.
7. One internal record is a breadcrumb, not the end of the trail.
8. A protected route can confirm that you are looking in the right place even before you can enter it.
9. Encoded data is not encrypted data. Identify the representation before treating it as a password.
