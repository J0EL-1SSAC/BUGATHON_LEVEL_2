# BUGATHON 2026 — Level 2: The Developer’s Mistake

**Difficulty:** Intermediate · **Objective:** complete the Level 2 investigation.

You begin immediately after Level 1 with a low-privilege `www-data` foothold on the
LPHU challenge VM. A registrar system was deployed with an operational mistake that
could expose far more than intended. Follow the evidence from the host to the internal
application, then determine what the application is really doing with your input.

The internal system is deliberately not externally published. Treat the local host,
service configuration, and accounts you discover as part of the investigation.

Rules:

- No brute force, wordlists, password cracking, kernel exploits, or external targets.
- Use only the fictional challenge environment and the access you earn during play.
- Checkpoint flags are proof of progress; preserve them as you find them.
- The final checkpoint also contains the information needed to start Level 3.

Ask an organizer for progressive hints if you are stuck.
