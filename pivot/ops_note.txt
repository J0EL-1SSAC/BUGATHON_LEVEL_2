IT Operations handoff — Registrar connectivity

The records portal is not published through Apache. Its service is named
level2-records.service and exposes a protected local Unix socket:

  /run/l2portal/l2portal.sock

Staff should use their SSH account to forward that socket to a local browser port.
Once connected, review /orientation before performing a student lookup.
